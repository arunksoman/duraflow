---
title: Round-tripping
description: What survives loading a document onto the canvas and saving it again.
---

Because the canvas is rebuilt from the DSL and written back to it, a document goes through a
round trip every time you open and save a workflow. Duraflow is built so that a hand-written
document comes back the way it was written, as long as it's valid.

## Preserved

- **Task names**, exactly, when they are identifier-shaped (`processElectronicOrder`, `send_email`).
- **Task order**, and the nesting of every body.
- **Every task type and its fields**, including the ones the panels don't have a field for:
  `metadata` other than activity options, and `input`/`output`/`export` schemas are carried along
  unchanged.
- `output.as` / `export.as` written as objects (they're shown and saved as JSON text).
- The **document header**: `title`, `summary`, `tags` and `metadata` (including workflow-wide
  `activityOptions` and `canMaxHistoryLength`).
- **Named workflows**, in the list they were declared in.
- **Switch cases**, in order, including a `then:` that names something the canvas can't resolve.
- **Fork branches** written as a single task or as a `do:` list.
- The **schedule**, and the Start node's `init` variables.

## Normalised

Some things are written in a canonical form, which means the same thing to Zigflow:

- Defaults are left out: a REST call's `output: content` and `redirect: false`, a fork's
  `compete: false`, zero-valued wait fields.
- Schema defaults the parser fills in on load are written explicitly where the builder needs them,
  such as `for.each: item` and `catch.as: error`.
- A child workflow's custom input is written under `input.value`.
- Comments and YAML formatting (quoting, key order within a task) are not preserved.
- Named workflows are written after the steps of their list, wherever they were in it.

## Not representable

A few things the DSL allows can't be drawn, and the builder avoids producing them:

- A `do:` that comes after another task is always a named workflow, never an in-place group, so a
  group can only be the first task of its list.
- A switch case's `then:` naming an ordinary sibling task (rather than a named workflow) is kept
  and drawn, but Zigflow can't run it.
- `call: activity` is not supported: there is no node for it, and the canvas would treat it as a
  REST call. Don't load documents that use it into the builder.

## Checking it yourself

Open the DSL editor, paste your document, and look at the canvas; then **Reset** reloads the text
from the canvas, which is exactly what will be saved. The round-trip behaviour is covered by the
engine's tests, including a load-and-save of `example/switch.yaml` from the repository.
